1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how PepTech Partners ("PepTech," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects personal information. PepTech operates its partner website, peplogicspeptides.com, and related pages, forms, partner portals, and communications (together, the "Services"). PepTech works with businesses: clinics, physicians, med spas, medical practices, research product buyers and resellers, brand founders, and affiliates.

2. What this policy covers

This policy covers information we collect through the Services, including when you visit the website, submit a partner application, use our partner portal, or communicate with our team.

Clinical information is handled separately. Telehealth and regenerative medicine services offered through partner programs are delivered by independent, licensed healthcare providers and their practices. Patient health information that those providers collect is protected health information under the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) and is governed by that provider's Notice of Privacy Practices, not this policy. Where PepTech handles protected health information on behalf of a provider, we do so as a business associate under a written business associate agreement and only as that agreement allows.

This policy also does not cover information collected by our white-label partners on their own websites or stores. Their privacy policies apply there.

3. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Partner application: first and last name, business name, work email, phone number, state, business type, the programs you are interested in, and your business stage.
  • Brand preview: if you type a brand name into the label preview on our website, we include it with your application so our team can prepare for your call.
  • D2C requests: first name, email address, and state, if you ask us to send you a link to a consumer site.
  • Onboarding and partnership: business details, licensing or credential information where a program requires it, tax and billing information, shipping addresses, branding files, and order history.
  • Communications: the content of emails, calls, texts, and messages you exchange with us. Calls may be recorded for quality and training where the law allows and with any notice the law requires.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and usage data: IP address, browser and device type, operating system, pages viewed, links clicked, the page that referred you, and the date and time of your visit.
  • Campaign data: marketing parameters in the link you used (such as utm_source or utm_campaign), which we attach to your application so we know which campaign brought you to us.
  • Pre-filled links: if you open a personalized link we sent you, it may contain your name, email, phone, company, or business type so the application form is filled in for you. That information comes from records you previously shared with us.

Information from other sources

We may receive business contact information from referral partners and affiliates, event organizers, public business directories, and licensing databases used to verify credentials.

4. How we use information

  • To review your application, contact you, and decide whether a partnership is a fit.
  • To onboard partners, give access to the partner portal, set up programs, process orders, and arrange warehousing, fulfillment, and shipping.
  • To verify licenses, credentials, and eligibility for programs that require them.
  • To send service messages, such as order updates, account notices, and policy changes.
  • To send marketing about our programs, where permitted. You can opt out at any time (see section 6).
  • To understand how the website and campaigns perform and to improve them.
  • To track affiliate referrals and pay commissions.
  • To prevent fraud, protect the Services, enforce our Terms of Use, and meet legal, tax, and regulatory obligations, including restrictions on research use only products.

5. How we share information

We do not sell personal information for money. We share information only as described here:

  • Service providers who work for us under contract, such as our customer relationship management system (Salesforce), our form-to-email delivery service (FormSubmit), website hosting, email and text delivery, scheduling, payment processing, warehousing, and shipping carriers. They may use the information only to provide services to us.
  • Independent healthcare providers and clinical partners, when you join a telehealth or regenerative medicine program and the information is needed to set up or run that program.
  • Affiliates and referral partners, limited to what is needed to credit a referral (for example, that a referred business applied or became a partner).
  • Corporate transactions: if PepTech is involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, information may transfer as part of that transaction.
  • Legal and safety: when we believe in good faith that disclosure is required by law, subpoena, or court order, or is needed to protect the rights, property, or safety of PepTech, our partners, or others.
  • With your consent or at your direction.
